# David Craft Ale — Design System

## Brand
- Name: DAVID CRAFT ALE ("Brewing Co." on labels). Est. 2023.
- Logomark: 8-point star. **Shape assigns the role, colour confirms it.** Both marks are
  generated by `starPath()`: `starSVG()` draws the 8-point logomark, `sparkleSVG()` draws
  a thin 4-point sparkle (`starPath(12,12,11,4,0.2)`). They are different marks and must
  not be used interchangeably:

  1. *Identity* — **cocoa `#572010` only**. The logo lockups, the menu and footer marks,
     the splash loader. Never animated, never scattered, never more than one per lockup.
  2. *Bullet* — a beer accent at 10–16px, sitting inline before a label (product chips,
     spec lists). Inherits the colour of the thing it belongs to.
  3. *Rating glyph* — the four-star row; the active beer's index takes `--beer-ink`, the
     rest take `--ink`.
  4. *Separator* — between marquee items and in `.brew-orn` rules.
  5. *Ambient sparkle* — **the 4-point `sparkleSVG()` mark, never the 8-point logomark.**
     A beer accent (or `--cream-soft` on dark grounds), 24–40px, may twinkle or spin
     slowly. **Budget: one per band or module**, tucked into a different spot each time —
     never a repeated corner formula. It carries ambient motion in sections Hopper was
     deliberately pulled from.

  The 8-point mark is the brand and appears only in roles 1–4. The 4-point sparkle is
  decoration and appears only in role 5. Cocoa belongs to role 1 alone.

  *This was written after an audit, and the audit found the build was not following it:*
  `icon-hero-star` and `icon-reviews-star` were drawing the 8-point logomark as ambient
  decoration while `icon-story-star` and `icon-affiliate-star` used the sparkle; the
  plushie stage and the product quick-view each carried four sparkles in a four-corner
  formula; and `quick-view-star qs-1` was cocoa. All six were corrected to match this
  spec. Ambient placements on index.html went from eight to three.

  *Known deviation, still open:* three places use the typographic glyph `✦` (U+2726)
  instead of `sparkleSVG()` — `.story-accent`, `.brew-card-spark` and `.legal-hero::after`,
  where it renders up to 74px. A third rendering of the same idea; fold it into
  `sparkleSVG()` or give it a declared role.
- Mascot: **Hopper** — a flying hop cone. Flat print-style character: plump teardrop hop
  cone body with overlapping scales, two big leaf-wings, stubby arms, dot eyes, tiny smile.
  Drawn in 2–3 flat colors (cocoa ink + one beer accent + cream), no gradients, no outlines
  thinner than 2px, slight vintage print misregistration is welcome.

  **Two sanctioned scales** (revised — the original spec allowed only the ambient scale):
  1. *Ambient* — 24–56px, drifting or hovering, used repeatedly across sections. This is
     the default and must stay the most common appearance.
  2. *Anchor* — one hero-scale placement per page (96–190px), animated, paired with the
     primary CTA. Exactly one may exist; it is the character's introduction.

  Rationale for the revision: at ambient scale alone the mascot read as clip-art rather
  than an ownable character, which failed the brief's core goal. Never a large **static**
  illustration — the anchor instance must animate.

color; colored display text on cream must use the "ink" variant of the beer color (AA ≥3:1
at display sizes, ≥4.5:1 at body sizes).

## Type
- Display: 'Alfa Slab One' — all headings + beer names + review-card quotes, **uppercase**
  (quotes are treated as heading-weight statements, not body copy — this has been the
  case since the reviews section was built and is deliberate, not an oversight).
  Line-height ≤1.1, clamp() scaling: hero 34–64px, beer name 46–84px, quote 16–20px,
  billboard/ghost 84–240px.
  The one sanctioned exception: the statement band's stat values ("15%", "30 days",
  "Paid monthly") use the display face at natural sentence case, since they sit inline
  with a small sans caption on the same pill rather than standing alone as a statement.
- Body/UI: 'Hanken Grotesk' — 15.5px body, 1.55 line-height.
- Wordmark: 'Archivo' 900 "DAVID" + 600 tracked "CRAFT ALE" — sans-serif, distinct from
  the Alfa Slab One display face; do not conflate the two when auditing type.
- Kickers: 13px, 800, uppercase, letter-spacing ≥0.2em, cocoa-soft.
- Fonts are embedded in fonts.css (base64 woff2) — no external requests.

## Components
- Buttons: pill (999px), uppercase 15px/800 tracked; primary solid cocoa/cream text;
  on-dark variant cream/cocoa. Hover: darken + translateY(-2px). Min target 44px.
- Bottles: real product photography (`assets/bottle-{id}.png`, background-removed
  cutouts), rendered via `bottleImg()` as `<img class="bottle-photo">` with
  `filter:drop-shadow(...)` for grounding. (Revised: replaced the parameterized inline-SVG
  bottle — the flat vector could not compete with a photographed product object on
  craft, and per-beer color-swap is now handled by the label art itself rather than
  CSS fill parameters.)
- Stars row: four 8-point stars; the active beer's index is the active beer's own ink
  color (`--beer-ink`, updated live on selection), rest `--ink` (body-text token, not
  `--cocoa` — this is a real, long-stable distinction: `--cocoa` is reserved for
  wordmark/bars/buttons, `--ink` for everything else that isn't actively highlighted).
- Beer selector: a **named** chip per beer — colour swatch + beer name (display face) +
  style (small sans), pill-shaped, wrapping to a 2×2 grid on mobile. All four are always
  visible. Active = paper bg + beer-ink border + shadow.
  (Revised: this replaced a 64×80 bottle-thumbnail rail, which showed no names and left
  a visitor seeing one beer instead of a range of four.)
- Toasts: cocoa pill, bottom-center, brand-voiced copy.
- Merch feature (Plushie): a full-bleed vintage-poster-frame module, distinct from the
  bottle/product-grid language on purpose — a single collectible item gets a one-off
  treatment, not the beer system's chip/tab pattern. Bordered `.plushie-frame` (2px
  cocoa rule, inset from the section edge) with a single ambient sparkle in the corner
  the badge and floating chips leave clear (revised from 4 corner glyphs — a repeated
  corner formula, and over the one-per-module budget); a circular badge
  (product name + "Limited Edition") sitting top-left of the frame, normal flow on
  mobile / absolutely pinned to the frame corner from 860px up; product cutout photo;
  copy column with line–star–line dividers above and below the two-tone headline,
  reusing the `.wordmark` lockup and a standard pill CTA. Photo gets a slow bob loop,
  same ambient-motion budget as the mascot.

## Motion
- Easing: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). Beer switch: fill/color transitions .5–.6s;
  bottle swap keyframe .5s.
- Ambient loops (marquee, mascot flight, badge rotation, hero video) must loop seamlessly.
- Scroll entrances: once per element, 400–700ms, slide-fade up.
- Everything honors prefers-reduced-motion (freeze loops, skip entrances).

## Voice
Warm, quirky, beer-punny but never sloppy. Utility copy carries the voice
("The bottle shop opens soon. First round's on us!"). Always ends with the legal footer
trio: Terms / Cookies & Privacy / Drink Responsibly. 18+.

## Media
- Generated stills/loops (Higgsfield / Seedance 2.5) must match the flat vintage-print
  illustration style: cream paper background, flat shapes, cocoa ink, beer-color accents,
  NO photorealism, no 3D render look, no drop shadows inside artwork.
- Videos: muted, autoplay, loop, playsinline; poster frame supplied; ≤8s; no burned-in text.

# Offer pop-up reference mechanisms

- The pop-up reads as a compact illustrated card: one strong headline, one short supporting line, the offer interaction, then one clear action.
- The hop texture covers the full surface but is muted enough that cocoa text remains the first thing seen.
- The product choices are shown as three equal rounded windows; the middle window is the active prize and receives the strongest contrast.
- Hopper breaks the right edge of the composition and acts as a visual anchor without covering the headline or primary action.
- The interaction has one obvious state change: idle selection, short spin, resolved reward. Motion ends in a stable readable result.
- The primary action is a cocoa pill at least 44px tall; secondary controls are visually quieter.
- On narrow screens the composition becomes a compact bottom sheet: Hopper shrinks, choices remain legible, and the close control stays reachable.

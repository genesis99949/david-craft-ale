Privacy & cookies

A plain-language explanation of what this fictional portfolio website does—and, importantly, what it does not do.

On this pageQuick summaryPersonal dataCookiesCart demoThird partiesChanges
Portfolio disclaimerDavid Craft Ale is a fictional project. There are no real customers, orders, subscriptions or commercial accounts behind this website.

Quick summary

The current local portfolio build does not intentionally collect, sell or share personal information. It contains no account registration, payment processing, mailing-list submission or analytics integration.

Personal data

The interactive elements are front-end demonstrations. Buttons may update the interface or show a message, but they do not submit your name, email address, payment information or other personal details to a business.

Cookies

The current project does not set advertising or profiling cookies. If analytics, embedded media or a hosting platform are introduced for a public release, this notice should be updated to identify those services and the choices available to visitors.

Cart demonstration

The cart on the Products page exists to showcase interaction design and front-end state management. Cart contents remain part of the browser demonstration and are not transmitted as a purchase.

Third-party resources

The website may load typography or media resources depending on its hosting configuration. Those providers can operate under their own privacy terms. The final published build should list every active third-party service here.

Future changes

This notice describes the current conceptual build. If contact forms, analytics, hosting telemetry or other data-processing features are added, the notice must be revised before those features are presented as active.

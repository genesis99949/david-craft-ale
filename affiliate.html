A few things to know

All clear?
Cheers.

Good partnerships start with a little clarity.

Can I apply to become an affiliate?

This is a fictional portfolio concept, so applications are not open. The page explores how a David Craft Ale partner experience could look and feel.

Are there commissions or referral rewards?

There is no operating affiliate program, referral tracking or payout system. Any example commercial figures elsewhere in the portfolio are fictional and are not an offer.

What’s inside the downloadable kit?

Four bottle images, the Hopper mascot, the brand mark and a short guide to the concept’s colours and voice. The ZIP is a local download, with no email address required.

Who is this concept designed for?

Adult creators, food and culture storytellers, and craft beer enthusiasts. The brand’s world is intended for an adult audience and encourages responsible enjoyment.

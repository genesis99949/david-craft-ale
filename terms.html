Concept terms

Simple terms for exploring a fictional craft-beer brand built as a graphic-design and front-end development portfolio project.

Portfolio disclaimerDavid Craft Ale and every product shown on this website are fictional. Nothing on this site constitutes a real commercial offer.

The concept

This website presents an imaginary brand created to demonstrate visual identity, packaging direction, responsive interface design, motion and front-end development. Product names, descriptions, reviews, locations and availability statements are part of the creative concept.

Using the site

You may browse the website and interact with its demonstrations for personal viewing and portfolio evaluation. Please do not use the website to misrepresent David Craft Ale as an operating brewery or retailer.

Orders and pricing

Prices, carts, product selectors and calls to action are interface demonstrations only. No order is accepted, no payment is processed and no product will be shipped. The drinking-age references are visual brand details rather than an age-verification service.

Creative work

The brand identity, layouts, illustrations, product renders, copy and code are presented as portfolio work. They may not be republished or presented as another person’s work without permission.

External links

If external references are added later, they will be provided for context. Their content and availability are controlled by their respective owners.

Contact and changes

This conceptual notice may evolve as the portfolio project develops. Contact information can be added when the website is prepared for public publication.
